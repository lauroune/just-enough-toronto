## Stuck points
Both are the same cause. Queen Street East crosses the Don on the bridge (c-2_0, x -817..-582). The authored Queen East physics (`src/physics.ts` line 26) puts a kinematic parked-tram collider, a 30.2 m by 2.5 m cuboid centred at (-600, 1.9, 1.55), across the road lane the router uses (z about 0.6). Route eastbound/westbound at z about 0.7 hits it: the probe at the stuck position (car at -617.1, 0.7, speed 0.09) found that cuboid inside a 3.5 m sphere (kinematic body, wall collision group), with no building and no terrain gap nearby (ground height is smooth, 1.71 at x -617, no jumps; nothing in `GEO.buildings` or downtown footprints spans the road there).
- Recommended fix (owner: authored-map / physics owner, `src/physics.ts` tram + `src/geo-world.ts` line 229 tram mesh): move the parked tram off the driving lane or exclude its collider in Rush mode (Rush has its own cars). Queen's lane in the route data is z 0.6 to 0.7, tram occupies z 0.3 to 2.8. Not a data or kit problem. The router could alternatively be steered to the other carriageway, but Queen is one 13 m wide road in the data.
- The drive script only counts a stuck point after 3 s below 0.8 m/s, then teleports 25 m ahead. Nothing else stopped the car anywhere on the other ~13 km.
