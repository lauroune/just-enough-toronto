Tree Small02 leaf atlas — Rico Cilliers / Poly Haven
https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02
CC0 https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
1K diffuse converted to JPEG, alpha unchanged. A leaf sample is composed into
shared branch cards at runtime. Placement and canopy shapes follow local map
points and video observations; the scan is not a botanical identification of
Toronto trees. No full high-poly tree model is included.

Toronto material patches — Mike Murchison (@mimurchison)
Mike Murchison’s own photo library and recorded videos informed the local
materials and architecture. Only cropped surface textures are bundled;
original footage, audio, people and vehicle plates are not included.
Camera colours and repeated approximate scale are not calibrated scans.

River of Life — full mural-bearing underpass wall
Lead artist: Quentin “Que Rock” Commanda. Contributions: Alex “Vizsla” Bacon, Sadie Marshall. Mentorship: Shawni-Nodin Nanaokase.
BIA photo: https://riverside-to.com/river-of-life-mural-celebration-highlights/
Artist film: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Csu9fJejSAU
Feature-aligned documentary projection and exposure matching. No generated artwork. 6144×1400 resampled documentary panorama, including expanded heron/fish/river sections. Source flare, alignment seams and small cropped extremities remain. Exact future wall extent is not surveyed.
Original image and art rights remain with their creators. Not a CC0 asset. Full film and original reference images are not shipped.
