# Toronto acrylic landscape

Created with the built-in image generation tool, using Mike's approved acrylic homepage concept as the reference. The final asset keeps the CN Tower, Queen Street streetcar, green trees, books and connecting lake strokes. All website lettering was removed so the page uses real, accessible HTML text.

Cast shadows outside the paint were removed, including the grey halo around the CN Tower observation deck. Pigment grooves remain within the paint. The artwork stays static, with no CSS shadow, filter or cursor motion.

`toronto-acrylic.avif` is 1494 × 1052 pixels, with alpha, quality 72 and 4:4:4 colour sampling. It is embedded alongside the fonts in the render-blocking stylesheet. The connected composition sits around desktop text and below the text on narrow screens.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: background-extraction / precise-object-edit.
The input is an approved website mockup. Extract its painted decorative landscape as a production website asset. Preserve EXACTLY the subjects, composition, colours, silhouette, position, proportions, and thick heavy-body acrylic brush texture: CN Tower, small skyline and streetcar in the LEFT margin; green trees and books in the RIGHT margin; long blue/lilac/coral/ochre acrylic sweep along the BOTTOM. Keep the same overall landscape aspect ratio and the completely empty central reading column.

MANDATORY EDITS:
1. Remove ALL text, letters, icons, rules, footer, and other website UI. These are added separately in HTML. The central area must be fully transparent.
2. Remove ALL cast shadows outside the coloured paint. Especially remove the black/grey shadow halo left and below the CN Tower observation pod. The painting lies directly on the page, not like a raised cut-out object floating above it. No grey/black ambient-occlusion shadow around the tower, trees, buildings, paint edges or books. Background immediately beyond each paint silhouette is fully transparent. Do not keep ghost shadows in the alpha.
3. Replace the white background with genuine transparency, including spaces between trees and around every brushstroke.

Retain opaque acrylic pigment, tangible bristle grooves and palette-knife ridges WITHIN the painted forms. Soft even frontal illumination, matte finish. Dimensional brush texture comes only from variations in the pigment colour. Do not add white glare, dark outlines, grey halos, watercolor washes or paper grain. Preserve the approved substantial buttery paint, do not flatten the material into vector art.
Output only the standalone transparent painted landscape frame. No white canvas, no text, no new objects, no checkerboard baked into the pixels. Maintain the original spacious composition and relative placement, use the highest clean resolution you can.
